Common questions

Why was my payment held?expand_more

Payments get a risk score from 0 to 100. Very large amounts, a first payment to a new recipient, or a sudden burst of payments raise the score. At 70 or more the payment is held and no money moves until an analyst reviews it.

Can I undo a payment?expand_more

Completed payments can't be reversed. That's why you review each payment before sending, and why the recipient's name is shown before you confirm.

What if I think my account is at risk?expand_more

Open Security and switch on the freeze for any account, or freeze all of them at once. You can also sign out of all devices and change your password.

Is this a real bank?expand_more

No. Vaultline is a demonstration project. It holds no real money, so don't enter real banking details.
